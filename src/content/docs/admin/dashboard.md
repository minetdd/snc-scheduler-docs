The dashboard gives you a quick read on your schedule every time you log in.

## Pending bookings alert

If any clients have submitted bookings that haven't been confirmed yet, a warning banner appears at the top of the dashboard listing each one. You can **Confirm** or **Decline** directly from the banner — no need to navigate to the Bookings page for quick actions.

The banner disappears automatically once all pending bookings have been acted on.

## Next / Your appointment

A featured card below the alert shows your nearest upcoming booking — client name, service, date, time, and status. The card is colour-coded to the service type.

- When you have **two or more** upcoming bookings the label reads **Next appointment** with a forward arrow.
- When you have **exactly one** upcoming booking the label reads **Your appointment** with no arrow, since there is nothing after it.
- When you have **no** upcoming bookings an empty-state message is shown instead.

## Stat tiles

Five tiles sit below the featured card, each updating in real time when the page loads:

| Tile | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Non-cancelled bookings with a start time today |
| **Upcoming** | All future non-cancelled bookings (confirmed + pending) |
| **Pending** | Future bookings awaiting your confirmation |
| **This Week** | Total hours of booked time in the current week |
| **This Month** | Non-cancelled bookings with a start time in the current calendar month |

All counts are calculated using your configured timezone, so "today" always means today in your location — not UTC.

## Today's Schedule

Below the stat tiles, a **Today's Schedule** section lists every booking for the current day in chronological order. Each entry shows:

- Client name and status chip
- Service name
- Session location (if provided)
- Start and end time

The section is hidden on days with no bookings.
